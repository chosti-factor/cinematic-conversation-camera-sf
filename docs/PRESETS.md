# Presets

The seven looks you can choose with `sPreset` in the `[Director]` section of the INI.
Standard, Close and Room are CCC's own, kept as faithful to CCC as Starfield allows.
Stardust, Starborn, Constellation and Nebula are CCC SF's own, with a Starfield flavour: a
37.5 degree lens (CCC's presets use 45), room shots kept closer, and shots from the other
presets mixed in, moving. Stardust, Starborn and Constellation each mirror one of CCC's;
Nebula uses every shot in the library.

Last updated: 0.8.3 (2026-11-15).

## Notes

- **Shares are before the room has its say.** Room shots (Wide, Distant, Distant low,
  Master) stand only where the room measurement finds space for them on the conversation's
  side with both faces in view (`iRoomShotMinRoom`). Where it doesn't, the preset picks from
  its other shots, so indoors the wider presets lean more on their long shots, two-shots
  and singles. Any shot the obstacle check finds blocked is skipped the same way.
- **Nebula is the least tested.** Its rarer angles (low, high, dirty, profiles, the shoulder
  variants) come from CCC's library but no other preset uses them yet. The ground-level
  shot is left out until room measurement can place it.
- **The same in every preset:** emotion close-ups on lines with an expression (every one but
  Neutral and Friendly, half the time: `bEmotionCloseUps`, `iCloseUpChance`),
  reaction shots of you listening (`iReactionEvery`, `iReactionChance`, `fReactionSeconds`),
  close-ups of your expressions (`iPlayerCloseUpChance`), holding on the answer after a
  persuasion (`bPersuasionBeat`), and the plain over-the-shoulder shots for seated people
  and merchants.
- **Zooms are pushes.** Starfield's lens doesn't change during a shot, so a zoom in is done
  as a push in and a zoom out as a pull out.
- **Extreme close-ups are rarer than in CCC.** CCC offers them on lines the writers marked
  as intense; Starfield's lines carry no such strength, so their weight is cut from 75 to 12
  (Close and Starborn). Intense lines still get them through the emotion close-ups.
- **Your own mix.** The `[Shots]` section of the INI changes any preset: `CloseUp=60` sets a
  shot's weight (0 turns it off), `CloseUpMove=Push in`, `CloseUpMoveAmount=40`,
  `CloseUpMoveTime=6`. `fMaxCameraDistance`, `fMaxCameraDistanceExterior`,
  `fRoomShotMaxIndoors`, `fRoomShotMaxOutdoors` and `fConversationLens` set your own
  distances and lens.
